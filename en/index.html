NeurIPS 2026Poster · Accepted

RIGOR: Risk-Gated Topology Adaptation for Robust LLM Multi-Agent Reasoning

Fengyuan Ran, Yikuan Wang*, Yanming Li, Wenjie Lu, Tongtong Wu, Senquan Yi, Yuheng Wang, Qiqi Lin, Yuxin Wu, Minghui Zhou, Naiqiang Tan, Li Shen

* Co-first author

RIGOR combines runtime risk inference and communication-graph editing with CVaR-guided training to improve multi-agent reasoning under query-varying misleading-message attacks. Evaluated across six benchmarks and two backbones, it achieves an average 21.09% accuracy improvement over the strongest baseline on Qwen3.5-35B-A3B.
